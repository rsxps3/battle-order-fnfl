Privacy

“Vaincre ou sombrer”

This page explains which personal data is used on this site (the battle order editor and the pages it publishes), where it comes from, who can access it and how to exercise your rights. This is a translation of the French version (Confidentialité), which is the original text.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Why this data is used

Purpose

To let you prepare a battle order and share it by link. The data is used for no other purpose.

Legal basis

Battle order content: service requested by the person using it (creating the link) and the unit’s legitimate interest in organising its games for the nicknames that appear in it. Technical logs and rate limiting: legitimate interest in ensuring the security and proper operation of the service.

No profiling

No automated decisions, no advertising, no resale of data.

Recipients and hosting

The site and the publishing service run on a virtual private server (VPS) hosted by IONOS, which technically processes the data on behalf of the unit.

  • A published page is visible to anyone who knows its link. The link is long and impossible to guess, and the pages are not indexed by search engines, but anyone you send the link to can pass it on.
  • No other recipient: the data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties.

Your rights

Access and rectification

You can ask which data concerns you and have inaccurate data corrected.

Erasure

You can ask for the deletion of a page that concerns you or of your data.

Objection and restriction

You can object to your name or nickname being displayed, or ask for its use to be restricted.

Portability

You can ask to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format. A battle order can also be exported at any time from the editor.

To exercise your rights, write to rsxps3.2@gmail.com. We reply within one month. The logs contain only an IP address: please give it to us so that we can find your data.
If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (its complaint service works in French): cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

Cookies and third-party services

This site uses no cookies, no audience measurement tools and no resources from a third-party site. The fonts are served by this site itself: your browser contacts neither Google Fonts nor any other service.
